Volta Confluence Engine — Every Part That Must Be Fabricated
VCE-MFR-001 Rev 1.0
CC BY-NC
Jake Galm, c. 2010
None

This register lists every component of the Volta Confluence Engine that must be fabricated, commissioned, or custom-wound rather than purchased off the shelf. For each part, the recommended fabrication method is stated along with the critical dimensions and tolerances. Machining instructions are listed in the companion document VCE-MACH-001.

All Machining Instructions Are Rough Draft

Per the standing project policy, all machining and fabrication parameters in this document and the companion machining instructions are labelled ROUGH DRAFT unless stated otherwise. Dimensions and geometries are correct. Feed rates, speeds, depths of cut, and toolpath strategies are indicative only and must be verified by a qualified operator with experience on the specific machine being used before cutting production parts. Do not treat any machining parameter as confirmed without that verification.

High-Voltage Safety — Read Before Fabricating Any Electrical Component

The capacitor bank operates at 600V DC. The valve anode circuits operate at up to 250V DC. These voltages are lethal at the available current levels. All high-voltage assembly, wiring, and commissioning must be performed by a qualified electronics technician. The builder must not energise any high-voltage circuit without a permanent bleed resistor (1MΩ, 5W) connected across the capacitor bank output. Panel interlocks disconnecting high voltage on access panel opening are mandatory, not optional. No physical prototype of this instrument has been built — treat all electrical specifications as engineering estimates requiring bench verification.

VCE-T02 / VCE-T03
Remontoire Spring Motor + Mainspring
Commission — Clockmaker · Qty 2

The remontoire spring motor is the most complex and most expensive commissioned part in the instrument. It cannot be self-built without specialist clockmaking skills and equipment. Commission from a precision clockmaker or specialist spring motor manufacturer. Lead time: 12–20 weeks. Cost: $3,000–$7,000 per unit.

The remontoire principle: the main spring drives a small secondary spring through a ratchet mechanism. The secondary spring is always wound to identical tension. The platter receives drive only from the secondary spring. The main spring rewinds the secondary every 8–10 seconds. Result: torque variation to platter <2% regardless of main spring state. A third maintaining power spring ensures the platter never hesitates during mid-play winding of the main spring.

High-carbon spring steel, precision ground Estimated
Mainspring dimensions

Barrel diameter
180mm outer Estimated
Remontoire spring
Beryllium copper, 40mm × 0.3mm Estimated
Remontoire cycle
8–10 seconds Estimated
Run time per wind
25–28 minutes (estimated — bench confirm on first article) Estimated
Output shaft speed
Appropriate for 33⅓ RPM at selected belt pulley position Rough Draft
Winding
Hand crank, 40 turns per side, maintaining power during winding Estimated
Commission Brief for Clockmaker Provide this specification sheet to the clockmaker. Key requirements: (1) remontoire principle mandatory — not a simple spring barrel; (2) maintaining power spring mandatory — platter must not hesitate during main spring winding; (3) output shaft must accept standard 5mm pulley bore for belt drive; (4) winding ratchet must accept external crank; (5) test for torque variation — must be <2% across full spring range. Request a test run time measurement on the completed unit before delivery.

VCE-T06
Mu-Metal Bearing Containment Ring
Lathe + Shear · Qty 2

A mu-metal annular ring encircling the magnetic bearing assembly. Its purpose is to reduce the stray field from the N52 bearing magnets to below 5 microtesla at cartridge position. Without this ring, the bearing magnets will induce hum in the moving coil cartridge that will be amplified by the horn system.

Material
Mu-metal (80% nickel, 15% iron alloy) — must be magnetically annealed after forming Confirmed
Thickness
1.5mm minimum — verify field attenuation by measurement before finalising Estimated
Height
Match bearing assembly height plus 10mm overlap top and bottom Rough Draft
Inner diameter
Clearance fit around bearing outer diameter — 2mm radial clearance minimum Rough Draft
Required field at cartridge
<5 microtesla — verify with gaussmeter before platter installation Estimated
Critical — Anneal After Forming Mu-metal loses most of its shielding effectiveness when cold-worked. After rolling or bending into ring form, the part must be annealed in a hydrogen atmosphere at 1,100°C (or equivalent). This requires specialist heat treatment. Source mu-metal sheet from a supplier who offers post-forming annealing, or arrange with a heat treatment contractor. Unnannealed mu-metal will not achieve the required field attenuation.
Section III

Cabinet & Woodwork

Commission Note — All Cabinet Work

All three cabinet commissions require an experienced cabinetmaker with acoustic woodworking knowledge. The corner horn enclosure is the most technically demanding — internal dimensions must be held to within 2mm of the calculated tractrix profile or the acoustic loading will not be correct. Provide the cabinetmaker with the full dimension drawings from the main document. The horn profile calculation is the builder's responsibility — the cabinetmaker executes the profile as drawn.

Section IV

Electrical & Electronics

Qualified Technician Required

All high-voltage electrical assembly must be performed by or under the direct supervision of a qualified electronics technician with experience in high-voltage DC circuits. This is not a beginner electronics project. The 600V capacitor bank stores enough energy to be lethal. The bleed resistor (1MΩ, 5W) must be permanently connected across the bank before any other connections are made. Test all circuits with a dummy load before connecting valves or speakers.

Section VIII

Isolation Platform

VCE-P01 / VCE-P02
Isolation Platform Brass Cups + Spring Housings
Lathe · Qty 8 each (4 per platform × 2 platforms)

The isolation platform sits under each turntable plinth. The brass cups contain precision steel ball bearings providing lateral isolation. The spring housings contain compression springs providing low-frequency vertical isolation. Together they target a 1.5Hz platform resonant frequency — well below any audio frequency and decisively below the acoustic feedback threshold.

Cup material
Brass CZ121 Estimated
Cup internal diameter
18mm — 17mm diameter steel ball bearing Estimated
Cup depth
9mm — ball sits half-proud for lateral freedom Estimated
Spring housing thread
M20 × 1.5mm — threaded height adjustment for levelling Rough Draft
Spring rate
Calculate from total plinth + slate + turntable mass for 1.5Hz resonance: k = m(2πf)² Calculated
Rough Draft — Spring Rate Must Be Calculated at Build Time Spring rate cannot be specified until the total mass resting on the platform is known. Weigh the assembled plinth, slate slab, and turntable. Calculate required spring rate for 1.5Hz resonance using k = m(2πf)². Source springs to that rate ±10%. Verify resonant frequency by gentle excitation of the assembled platform — the platform should oscillate slowly and damp within 3–4 cycles.
